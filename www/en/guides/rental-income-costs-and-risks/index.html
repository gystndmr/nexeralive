Buying to let: costs and risks.

Dividing the asking price by the expected rent gives a gross figure. To see the real return you must account for tax, running costs and the months the flat stands empty. There is no such thing as a guaranteed yield.

Last reviewed: 6 November 2026 · Prepared by: NEXERA İnşaat

SHORT ANSWER

Net yield = (annual rent − vacancy − service charges, maintenance, insurance, property tax and income tax) ÷ total purchase cost. In 2026 the first TRY 58,000 of annual residential rent is exempt under conditions; rent above that is declared and taxed at 15%–40%. Non-residents must also declare rent from Türkiye. Renting a home to tourists for up to 100 days requires a permit from the Ministry of Culture and Tourism.

Gross and net yield

Gross yield = annual rent ÷ purchase price. Net yield deducts the items below and divides by the total purchase cost (including fees, VAT, valuation and so on):

  • Vacancy: allow for empty months between tenants.
  • Service charges, maintenance, repairs and furniture replacement.
  • DASK and optional home insurance.
  • Property tax: 0.2% of the tax value in Istanbul.
  • Income tax (below) and any agency or management fees.
  • Currency risk: rent is in lira; if you invested in foreign currency, your return moves with the exchange rate.

Tax on rental income (2026)

TopicRule
ExemptionThe first TRY 58,000 a year of residential rent is exempt. The exemption is lost if income above it is not declared or is under-declared, and it does not apply to people who file returns for business, agricultural or professional income or whose total gross income exceeds TRY 1,500,000 in 2026.
ExpensesInstead of actual expenses you may deduct a flat 15% of the rent; once chosen, you must keep this method for 2 years.
RatesProgressive: 15%–40%.
Non-residentsRent from property in Türkiye is taxable for non-residents too. Because private-individual tenants do not withhold tax, non-resident landlords in practice need to file a return.

Short-term (tourist) rentals

Since 1 January 2024, Law No. 7464 requires a permit from the Ministry of Culture and Tourism to rent a home to tourists for up to 100 days. All owners in the building must agree unanimously; a plaque is displayed at the door and guests are registered with the authorities. Renting without a permit is fined per home.

Residence permit and renting do not mix

For a property-based short-term residence permit, the Presidency of Migration Management requires the home to be used as the applicant’s residence and not rented out. If you plan both, discuss it with a lawyer before applying.

Checklist

  • Base your rent estimate on actual lettings of similar flats nearby; asking rents are not achieved rents.
  • Include the full purchase costs in your calculation.
  • Re-run the numbers assuming at least one empty month and one month of maintenance costs a year.
  • If you plan short-term lets, check in advance whether the building can meet the permit conditions.

This page is general information, not legal, financial or tax advice. Rules and amounts change; confirm the current position with the official authorities and an independent lawyer before any transaction.
