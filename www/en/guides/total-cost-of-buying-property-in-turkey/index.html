GUIDE · COSTS

The total cost of buying a home in Türkiye.

The price of the flat is only part of what you pay. The table below lists the items found in the official texts as of 2026 and who pays them. When you get an offer, ask in writing whether each item is included.

Last reviewed: 6 November 2026 · Prepared by: NEXERA İnşaat

SHORT ANSWER

For a resale home the main extra costs are the buyer’s 2% title-deed fee, the land registry service fee, DASK earthquake insurance and, for foreign buyers, the valuation report and translator. For the first sale of a new home, VAT is added: 10% on the first 150 m² net and 20% above for projects permitted from 1 April 2022, or 1% on the first 150 m² in urban-renewal projects. After that you pay annual property tax (0.2% of the tax value in Istanbul) and service charges.

VAT exemption for non-residents

Under article 13/i of the VAT Law, the first sale of a home or workplace to foreign individuals not resident in Türkiye, to Turkish citizens who have lived abroad for more than six months with a work or residence permit, and to companies resident abroad is exempt from VAT, provided the price is paid in foreign currency brought into Türkiye. If the property is sold within 3 years, the VAT not paid because of the exemption must be paid with late-payment interest before the title transfer, and the buyer is jointly liable if the conditions were not met. Confirm the documentation with a tax adviser before you buy.

Worked example (method only)

Assumption: a resale home with a declared price of TRY 10,000,000, above its property-tax value. Buyer’s title-deed fee: 10,000,000 × 2% = TRY 200,000. Add that year’s land registry service fee, the DASK premium and any valuation, translator and notary fees. For the first sale of a new home VAT is calculated separately; make sure the contract states whether VAT is included.

Questions to ask with every offer

  • Is the price VAT-inclusive? At which rate and on how many net m²?
  • Who pays the title-deed fee and the land registry service fee?
  • Are electricity, water and gas connections included?
  • When do service charges start, and roughly how much are they?
  • For off-plan purchases, which construction stages are the payments tied to?

This page is general information, not legal, financial or tax advice. Rules and amounts change; confirm the current position with the official authorities and an independent lawyer before any transaction.
